# Home

I'm a born and bred Oslovian, with a master's degree from the Department of Mathematics at the University of Oslo, where I specialized in aerodynamics and marine hydrodynamics.
In September I start a research master at the von Kármán Institute for Fluid Dynamics in Belgium.
I sing in [*Meraki Kammerkor*](https://merakikammerkor.no/) in my free time, and devote a lot of time to $\LaTeX$.

I've always had an interest in languages.
In fact, I'm now an armchair linguist, with particular interest in Latin and Slavic languages.

## Research interests
- Vortex lattice methods
- Fast multipole methods
- Geometric hydrodynamics
- Boundary layer theory
