# Home

I'm a born and bred Oslovian, currently finishing my master's degree at the Department of Mathematics at the University of Oslo, specializing in aerodynamics and marine hydrodynamics.
I sing in [*Meraki Kammerkor*](https://merakikammerkor.no/) in my free time, and devote a lot of time to $\LaTeX$.

I've always had an interest in languages, now being an armchair linguist, with particular interest in Latin and Slavic languages.

## Research interests
- Geometric hydrodynamics
- Fast multipole methods
- Vortex lattice methods
- Boundary layer theory
